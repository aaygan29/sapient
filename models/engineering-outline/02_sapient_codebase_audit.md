## TRIBE-specific gotchas to know for the Q&A

- **Feature extraction is decoupled.** TRIBE v2 doesn't train V-JEPA / Wav2VecBert / Llama. It runs them once and caches activations. If anyone asks "did you train Llama" — *neither did TRIBE*. Saying so cleanly is a sign of fluency.
- **The 8 transformer layers and ~1.1k hidden width fit on 1 GPU.** The capital-intensive part is feature extraction (Meta paper: 128 V100s × 24 h for v1), not model training (1 V100 × 24 h). Your one-A100 fine-tune story maps directly to TRIBE's own model-training step.
- **Loss is plain MSE.** Don't claim it's something exotic. Where TRIBE is sophisticated is in the data pipeline and subject handling, not the loss.
- **Subject dropout 0.1 = zero-shot to new subjects.** This is the mechanism behind the blog's "zero-shot to new subjects" claim. Memorize it.
- **No HRF learning.** TRIBE assumes the canonical 5-TR offset. A learned-HRF architecture is a credible Sapient differentiator if you have it.
- **fsaverage5 has 20,484 vertices.** "~20k" is the marketing round; if pressed, the real number is 20,484 (10,242 × 2 hemispheres).

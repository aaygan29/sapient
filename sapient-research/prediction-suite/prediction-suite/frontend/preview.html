Sapient · Prediction Suite

From predicted brain response
to forecast behavior.

Mary predicts how a brain responds to your content. This layer turns that response into a calibrated forecast of what the market will do — grounded in the published neuroforecasting paradigm that predicted a 400,000-person campaign from 50 brains, and gated so it never reports a number the neural signal can't carry.

Grounded in the neuroforecasting literature

Knutson et al., Neuron 2007. NAcc / MPFC / insula predict purchases beyond self-report.

Falk et al., Psych Science 2012. 50 brains forecast a 400,000-person national quit-line campaign; self-report did not.

Kühn et al., NeuroImage 2016. fMRI forecast real chocolate sales across 63,617 shoppers; signed region directions.

Genevsky et al., PNAS Nexus 2025. Brain forecasts stay valid on non-representative samples where behavior forecasts break.
